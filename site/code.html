the code

Every Vinha dos Padres label carries a short code in the top left corner of the front label. It tells you, in months, how long the wine spent macerating on its skins within the amphora (talha) and how many total months it spent aging in the same amphoras (talhas). An additional “M” will detail an additional aging period in a different vessel. Vinha dos Padres is at the forefront of scientifically investigating the use of old amphoras and their winemaking qualities.
